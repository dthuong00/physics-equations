The world's most famous equation

E = mc² is only
a special case.

E² = (pc)² + (mc²)²
true for everything - electrons, planets, light and E = mc² is only what is left when p = 0

It is exactly right for one situation and silent about every other: the object has to be standing still. The moment anything moves, you need the rule above it - and that rule is where light speed, solar sails, and 99% of your own body weight come from.

The same one line, read three ways

momentum p = 0
E = mc²

Nothing is moving, so the motion term vanishes and the famous equation is what is left. One kilogram at rest holds 9×10¹⁶ J - about 21 megatons of TNT.

both terms at once
E² = (pc)² + (mc²)²

Every real electron, proton and rocket. The motion term can be driven as high as you like and never erases the mass term.

mass m = 0
E = pc

No mass term at all, so E = pc - which forces the speed to be exactly c. Massless things get no choice about speed, and carry momentum with no mass, which is how sunlight pushes a solar sail.

One curve, and E = mc² is a single point on it

The whole relation is this one curve. E = mc² is a single point on it - the tick on the E axis, where momentum is zero. The curve leaves that point horizontally, because the slope is β, and β = 0 for something at rest.

The dashed line is E = pc - the massless case, at exactly 45° because β = pc/E is 1 there. Photons ride on it. Anything with mass stays above it forever: by pc = 5mc² the gap has shrunk tenfold, to a tenth of mc², and it keeps shrinking without ever closing. Below the line there is nothing - that would need an imaginary mass.

Four things E = mc² cannot tell you

Why can nothing with mass reach light speed?

A particle’s speed is pc/E - and E stays permanently above pc unless m is zero. That is the gap in the chart: you close on c forever and never arrive.

How can light push a solar sail with no mass?

Set m = 0 and the relation collapses to E = pc. Momentum survives; the mass never had to be there. Light carries push without mass.

Where does 99% of your body weight come from?

Not the Higgs field. A proton weighs 938 MeV, its three valence quarks only 9. The rest is the energy of the gluon field that confines them.

How do you weigh a particle gone in 10⁻²² seconds?

You never see it - only its debris. Add the two fragments’ energies and momenta, then take √(E² − (pc)²) of the pair. Two massless photons can add up to a 125 GeV parent, and every real pair lands on the same number: a bump.

What mass actually is

(mc²)² = E² − (pc)²

Rearranged, it says what is real. E and pc are only components, different for every observer - the object they belong to is the four-momentum (E/c, p), and m is its length. A length does not change when you turn your head, which is why all three observers below write down the same 0.938.
