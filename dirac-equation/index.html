cannot outrun light.

Dirac asked for a quantum equation that is first-order in time, respects special relativity, and conserves probability. The answer forced spin and antimatter into the mathematics.

Summary
01

Two successful theories disagree on energy

Schrödinger uses the non-relativistic kinetic energy. Einstein says energy and momentum belong to a different geometry.

SCHRÖDINGER'S ENERGY RULE
E = p²/2m + V

The rule behind the Schrödinger equation. One branch, rising as the square of momentum with no ceiling — it works well at low speeds but is not compatible with special relativity.

but
EINSTEIN'S ENERGY RULE
E² = p²c² + m²c⁴

Two branches, separated by a gap of 2mc², each bending toward the dotted light line E = pc. No massive excitation reaches or crosses light speed.

Taking a square root gives E = ±√(p²c² + m²c⁴). Dirac wanted an equation linear in both energy and momentum without discarding either sign.

Now restore all three momentum directions

Important: bold α is not one matrix. It is shorthand for a collection of three matrices—one for each spatial direction.

Two three-item collectionsα = (α₁, α₂, α₃)    p = (p₁, p₂, p₃)

The dot product pairs matching items: α·p = α₁p₁ + α₂p₂ + α₃p₃.

Each direction squares correctlyα₁² = α₂² = α₃² = I

This makes the three main terms become p₁²I, p₂²I and p₃²I.

Mass conditionsβ² = I   and   {αᵢ, β} = 0

The first matches the pure mass term. The second cancels each momentum–mass cross-term.

Physical requirementαᵢ† = αᵢ   and   β† = β

The dagger means conjugate transpose. These conditions make Ê Hermitian, giving real measured energies and probability-conserving evolution.

Why mixed terms cancelα₁α₂ = −α₂α₁

Reversing the order reverses the sign, so the two unwanted terms add to zero. The other matrix pairs behave the same way.

We need four 4 × 4 matricesα₁, α₂, α₃ and β

A 2 × 2 matrix can provide only three sign-reversing choices, so the smallest matrix that can provide all four is 4 × 4.

One wavefunction, four coupled amplitudes

What is an amplitude?

Each ψᵢ is a complex number with a size and phase. It is not a probability by itself; |ψᵢ|² contributes to the total probability.

Coupled = the components affect one another

If ψ₁ changes, the equation can make ψ₃ change too. Entries away from the matrix diagonal connect their equations, so ψ₁, ψ₂, ψ₃ and ψ₄ must be calculated together—not as four independent wavefunctions.

Spin

Intrinsic angular momentum appears without being added as a separate assumption.

Antimatter

The second energy branch becomes physical antiparticles, not a mathematical nuisance.

Conserved probability

ρ = ψ†ψ = |ψ₁|² + |ψ₂|² + |ψ₃|² + |ψ₄|² stays positive and obeys a local conservation rule.
