The Dirac equation

A quantum rule for very small particles moving near the speed of light.

iℏ ∂ψ/∂t = (cα·p + βmc²)ψ

1 · The problem Dirac wanted to solve

Schrödinger equation

tells us how a quantum wavefunction changes with time, but its usual form is not compatible with special relativity.

Special relativity

says energy and momentum must obey E² = p²c² + m²c⁴.

Dirac needed one equation that obeyed both theories—and used only a first time derivative, so the future state follows directly from the present state.

3 · Why do matrices appear?

1Start with relativity

E² = p²c² + m²c⁴

2Try a linear energy rule

Ê = cα·p + βmc²

3Square it

Wanted squares appear, plus unwanted mixed terms.

4Cancel the extras

Choose matrices that change sign when their order is reversed.

αᵢαⱼ + αⱼαᵢ = 0for different directionsαᵢβ + βαᵢ = 0for momentum and mass

In plain language: ordinary numbers give ab = ba, so the unwanted terms add. These matrices can give AB = −BA, so the pair cancels. Their squares equal the identity matrix I, which behaves like the number 1.

These are the same information repackaged: γ⁰ = β and γⁱ = βαᵢ. Multiplying the equation by β removes β from the mass term because β² = I. The simple rules are: (γ⁰)² = I, each space matrix squares to −I, and any two different gamma matrices reverse sign when their order is reversed.

5 · Why four components?

Three matrices are needed for momentum in the x, y and z directions. A fourth is needed for mass. A 2 × 2 system can fit only three mutually anticommuting matrices, so the smallest working system is 4 × 4.

ψ =( ψ₁   ψ₂   ψ₃   ψ₄ )ᵀ

The four entries form one object called a spinor. They are not four separate particles.

The four gamma matrices package the same structure: γ⁰ = β and γⁱ = βαᵢ for the three spatial directions.

6 · What falls out of the equation?

  • Spin: two possible spin states appear naturally.
  • Antimatter: the negative-energy branch predicts antiparticles.
  • Probability: Hermitian α and β keep total probability conserved.
  • Speed limit: signals do not outrun light.
  • Zitterbewegung: mixing energy branches can produce rapid trembling motion.
Big ideaDirac found a linear “square root” of Einstein's energy equation. The matrices required to make that square root work automatically brought spin and antimatter into quantum physics.
