01

Three ingredients. One relationship.

Acceleration is what you observe. Force and mass are the two knobs that decide it.

F

Net force

The combined push after opposing forces are subtracted. Measured in newtons (N).

m

Mass

How much matter resists a change in motion. Measured in kilograms (kg).

a

Acceleration

How quickly velocity changes. Measured in metres per second, every second (m/s²).

More net force → more acceleration. More mass → less acceleration.
